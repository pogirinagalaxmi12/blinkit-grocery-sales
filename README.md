BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis Dashboard
Project Overview
This project analyzes BlinkIT grocery sales data to understand sales performance across product categories, outlet types, outlet locations, outlet sizes, and product characteristics. The goal is to turn raw sales records into clear business insights that can support better business decisions.
Organization: Sapphire IQ
Domain: Retail & Grocery Sales Analytics
Dataset: BlinkIT Grocery Data

Business Objectives
Understand overall sales performance and the types of products and outlets in the dataset.
Review data quality and consistency.
Compare product and outlet performance to identify sales drivers.
Provide practical insights that may support sales improvement and business planning.
How to Use
Download or clone this repository.
Open BlinkIT_Grocery_Sales_Dashboard.xlsx in Microsoft Excel.
Select the Dashboard worksheet to view the KPIs and charts.
Use Cleaned Data to filter and inspect records.
Open Analysis to review the summary tables used by the dashboard.
Notes
The dashboard metrics are calculated from the supplied dataset.
The total sales value shown above is the sum of the dataset's Sales column.
The dashboard is a descriptive analysis of the supplied records; it does not establish causal relationships between product or outlet characteristics and sales.
